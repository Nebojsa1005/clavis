# Instrument samples

The `.mp3` files in this folder come from the **Fluid R3 GM** SoundFont (Frank Wen), as pre-rendered
by [gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts](https://github.com/gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts).

Licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/us/).
Only a subset of notes is included; the player pitches between them.

Regenerate with `node scripts/fetch-samples.mjs` (list in `src/app/core/models/instrument-sounds.json`).
